Acme Corp Remote Work and Device Security Policy (v3.2)
All remote employees must operate on company-issued workstations. Personal computing equipment (BYOD) is strictly prohibited from accessing internal code repositories and AWS production environments.
Disk encryption via BitLocker or FileVault must remain active at all times. Laptops must be configured with an automatic inactivity lock after 5 minutes of idle time.
Employees working from public locations such as cafes or airports must always use a privacy screen filter and establish network connections exclusively through the corporate WireGuard VPN tunnel.
Failure to adhere to device encryption standards results in immediate revocation of corporate credentials and incident reporting to the Infosec Operations Group.
