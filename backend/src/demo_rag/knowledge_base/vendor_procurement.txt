Acme Corp Vendor Procurement & Invoicing Policy
All software licensing purchases exceeding $5,000 must obtain prior budgetary approval from the department director and VP of Finance.
Invoices must be submitted in PDF format to accounts-payable@acme.internal with corresponding Purchase Order (PO) numbers clearly indicated in the header.
Standard vendor payment terms are Net-30 from invoice receipt date, unless customized in signed master service agreements.
Third-party contractors requiring access to Acme Corp source code repositories must sign NDA agreements and pass background screening before account provisioning.
Annual security reviews must be conducted for all SaaS vendors processing customer confidential information.
