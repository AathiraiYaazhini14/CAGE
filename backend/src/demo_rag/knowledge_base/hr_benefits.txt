Acme Corp Employee Benefits & Paid Time Off FAQ
Full-time regular employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation time (PTO) per calendar year, calculated at 1.67 days per month worked.
A maximum of 5 unused vacation days may roll over into the following calendar year. Any additional unused PTO expires automatically on December 31st.
Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance begins on the first day of the calendar month following employment commencement.
Acme Corp provides a 100% employer match for 401(k) retirement contributions up to 5% of gross base salary, vested immediately.
For parental leave, primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave, while secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks.
